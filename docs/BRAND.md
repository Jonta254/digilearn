# DigiLearn brand system

The DigiLearn symbol combines two open pages with a three-step path rising to the right. It represents structured knowledge, practice and forward progress without relying on a graduation cap, robot or decorative orbit.

## Assets

- `public/brand/digilearn-logo.svg`: horizontal logo for light backgrounds
- `public/brand/digilearn-logo-dark.svg`: horizontal logo for dark backgrounds
- `public/brand/digilearn-logo-mono.svg`: single-colour production
- `public/brand/digilearn-symbol.svg`: standalone symbol
- `public/brand/digilearn-profile.png`: square profile image

## Colour

- Deep ink: `#12304A` ? primary wordmark and symbol
- Progress teal: `#0F9FA8` ? learning path and focus
- Page mist: `#DCEBED` ? supporting page
- White: `#FFFFFF` ? reversed primary surface

These combinations meet strong contrast needs when ink is used on white and white is used on ink. Progress teal is an accent, not body text on white.

## Clear space and sizing

Keep clear space equal to one quarter of the symbol width on every side. Do not stretch, rotate, recolour individual paths or place the light logo on a light background.

- Standalone symbol: minimum 16px
- Compact navigation logo: minimum 30px high
- Horizontal logo: minimum 120px wide
- Print: minimum 24mm wide for the horizontal logo

For favicon and maskable icons, use the supplied derived assets; their padding is already tuned for clipping-safe rendering.
