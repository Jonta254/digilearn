# DigiLearn practical project brief

## Scenario
A fictional small organisation needs a reliable digital output that solves a clearly defined work problem.

## Objective
Build the course project using only fictional or openly licensed information.

## Deliverable
Submit a working output, a short explanation of your decisions, and evidence that you tested it.

## Success checks
- The output addresses the stated user need.
- Important assumptions and data sources are documented.
- Accessibility, privacy and safety checks are complete.
- Another person can reproduce the result from your notes.

## Extension
Adapt the project for a second realistic use case and explain what changed.
