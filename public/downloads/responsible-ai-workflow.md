# Responsible AI workflow

Course: ____________________  Date reviewed: __________

1. Source material: record its origin, owner and sensitivity.
2. Instruction: state the task, audience, limits and required format.
3. Draft output: save the unedited result separately.
4. Human review: check facts, omissions, bias and tone against the source.
5. Privacy check: remove personal, confidential and unnecessary data.
6. Verification: cite authoritative evidence and record corrections.
7. Approval: name the accountable reviewer; never imply automated approval.
